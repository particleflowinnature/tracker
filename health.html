What to Monitor common for germ-cell tumour survivors; your doctors decide what and when

At home (log here)

  • Weight, waist (90 cm or more is the NIN risk mark), blood pressure
  • Energy, sleep, water, digestion
  • Cough or breathlessness (past bleomycin), numbness or tingling in hands/feet, ringing in ears or hearing change (past cisplatin/paclitaxel)
  • A new lump, back/abdominal pain, or a testicular change: report promptly

Cancer surveillance (your uro-oncologist's schedule)

  • AFP, β-hCG, LDH
  • CT chest/abdomen; neck imaging if your oncologist advises

Blood work to ask your physician about

  • Vitamin B12 (was 173, deficient), with homocysteine or MMA; folate
  • 25-OH vitamin D (was 23.5), calcium
  • Magnesium and potassium (cisplatin can lower both), creatinine/eGFR
  • CBC and ferritin

Long-term health after chemo

  • Fasting glucose or HbA1c, lipid profile, BP (cardiometabolic risk)
  • Testosterone, LH, FSH (hypogonadism risk); TSH
  • Fertility and semen analysis if you plan children
  • Bone health if testosterone is low
